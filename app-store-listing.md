## Description

PDF Viewer & Editor is a fast, friendly way to read and work on PDF documents right on your iPhone.

Open a PDF from Files or iCloud Drive and it joins your library, reopening exactly where you left off. Read in continuous scrolling or one page at a time, jump with the thumbnail grid or the document's own table of contents, and find any word with full-text search.

When a document needs more than reading, the editor is right there:

FEATURES
• READ — smooth native rendering, pinch zoom, page indicator, bookmarks, favorites, and a recents library that remembers your place
• SEARCH — full-text search with a results list that jumps straight to each match
• HIGHLIGHT & UNDERLINE — mark up text in four colors
• DRAW — freehand ink in your choice of color and pen width, saved right into the document
• TEXT BOXES & STICKY NOTES — add your own words anywhere on the page
• FILL FORMS — tap and type into real PDF form fields, then save
• SIGN — draw your signature once, save it, and stamp it onto any page, positioned exactly where you want it
• MANAGE PAGES — reorder pages by dragging thumbnails, rotate, or delete pages
• MERGE — combine two or more PDFs into one new document
• SHARE — export your edited PDF anywhere with the standard share sheet. Your original files are never changed: the app always works on its own imported copy
• PRIVATE BY DESIGN — documents are stored only on your device. No accounts, no uploads, no analytics

FREE WITH ADS — SUPPORTED HONESTLY
The app is free, supported by one small banner at the bottom of the screen and one full-screen ad after about every five minutes of active reading. Prefer it clean? Remove Ads is a single one-time purchase (never a subscription) that turns every ad off forever.

PDF Viewer & Editor is an original, independent app. It is not affiliated with Adobe Inc.; PDF is an open standard (ISO 32000).
